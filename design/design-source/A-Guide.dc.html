6 min read · Last verified on 14 October 2026

In short

WhatTelling the city where you live. You get a paper you need for almost everything else. WhenEvery time you move into a new flat. DeadlineWithin 2 weeks of moving in
Ad [Native ad title][Advertiser · 1 line]

Steps

1

Get the landlord’s confirmation

Your landlord signs a form saying you moved in. Ask for it when you get the keys.

DEWohnungsgeberbestätigung
2

Book an appointment at the citizen’s office

Leipzig releases new slots online every morning. Any office in the city works.

Typical cost
Free
Typical wait
3–10 days
3

Go to the appointment

You leave with your registration certificate. Keep it safe — the bank and the immigration office will ask for it.

DEMeldebescheinigung

Documents to bring

Tip. No slots? Check the booking page right after 7:00 — cancelled slots come back then.
Common mistake. Waiting for a slot is not an excuse for missing the 2 weeks. Book on day one, even if the slot is later.
