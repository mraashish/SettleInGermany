In short

WhatTelling the city where you live. You get a paper you need for almost everything else. WhenEvery time you move into a new flat. DeadlineWithin 2 weeks of moving in

Steps

1

Get the landlord’s confirmation

Your landlord signs a form saying you moved in. Ask for it when you get the keys.

DEWohnungsgeberbestätigung
2

Book an appointment at the citizen’s office

Leipzig releases new slots online every morning. Any office in the city works.

Go to the appointment
