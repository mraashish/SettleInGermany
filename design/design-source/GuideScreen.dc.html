Register your address

DE Anmeldung
6 min read · Last verified on [date]

In short

WhatTelling the city where you live. You get a paper you need for almost everything else. WhenEvery time you move into a new flat. DeadlineWithin 2 weeks of moving in

Steps

1

Get the landlord’s confirmation

Your landlord signs a form saying you moved in. Ask for it when you get the keys.

DEWohnungsgeberbestätigung
2

Book an appointment at the citizen’s office

Leipzig releases new slots online. Any office in the city works.

Typical cost
Free
Typical wait
[X–Y days]
3

Go to the appointment

You leave with your registration certificate. The bank and the immigration office will ask for it.

DEMeldebescheinigung

Documents to bring

Tip. No slots? Check the booking page early in the morning — cancelled slots often come back then.
Warning. Moving again? You must register the new address too, and deregister when you leave Germany.
Common mistake. Waiting for a slot doesn’t pause the 2 weeks. Book on day one, even if the slot is later.
